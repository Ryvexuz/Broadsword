## 3. Item 4: shadow-finding verdicts need an independent judge (audit A8). DECISION FOR LAZLO

**The rule.** `SPEC_CHECKLIST.md:48` (Lazlo, 2026-11-18): every BLOCKING line only the shadow reviewer raised gets a verdict from "an independent strong judge (neither the coder nor the gating reviewer)", with evidence: real, false, dup or unsure.

**What the record shows.** The audit counted 24 rows (17 by the gating reviewer, 7 by the orchestrator; 10 real, 11 known, 3 false). On 2026-11-20 `state/shadow-verdicts.tsv` has 59 data rows, all dated 2026-11-19. Judge column, counted with `cut -f5 state/shadow-verdicts.tsv | tail -n +2 | sort | uniq -c`: `haiku (gating)` 20, `opus (gating)` 1, the orchestrator (13 plain, 25 with a note such as "Opus gate raised the same points") 38. Verdict column (`cut -f3 state/shadow-verdicts.tsv | tail -n +2 | sort | uniq -c`): real 33, known 23, false 3. No row has a judge that is neither the coder nor the gating reviewer. The harm the ledger row names (a Claude judge favouring Claude, three findings ruled `false` by the reviewer that had missed them) is still possible in every row.

**Option A: keep the rule, make the practice meet it.** Re-judge the recorded rows, and every new shadow-only BLOCKING line, with a judge that is neither the coder nor the gating reviewer (a different-family model or a different Claude model than both). Cost: one strong-model call per shadow finding; at the recorded rate (59 rows in one day) that is the largest recurring review cost of the pipeline; it can be sampled (all `false` and `real` rows, since `known` rows only point at an earlier finding).

**Option B: change the rule to what is practised.** The gating reviewer (or the orchestrator, when the gate did not judge) gives REAL, FALSE or KNOWN with evidence, and the report measures the shadow's precision and the gate's misses from those rows. Add a recall guard: a fixed random sample (for example one in five `false` verdicts) goes to an independent judge, so a systematic bias shows up. This needs a ledger row in `RETRO.md` under the Self-optimization rule, and Lazlo's OK, since it touches the rule list.

**Recommendation: Option B with the sample guard.** The rule as written has not been followed since 2026-11-18 and cannot be at 59 rows a day; a rule that is never met is a wish (playbook lesson 28). Option B states the real practice, keeps one independent check where the bias would matter (a `false` ruling by the reviewer that missed the point), and costs a few calls, not one per finding. If Lazlo prefers A, the `false` and `real` rows are the first to re-judge.

**DECIDED by Lazlo, 2026-11-21.** On 2026-11-21 at 00:50Z Lazlo answered "Sure" to the coordinator's 00:47Z post, which recommended Option B (source: the coordinator's message to this task; I did not see the post or the reply myself).

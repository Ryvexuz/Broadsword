## 5. Recommendation (self-optimization rule; no rule changed)

**Keep astra as a standing second sweep, but run it once per stage end (not twice) and triage by severity; do not let it replace the Claude auditor.** Numbers: astra found 189 distinct REAL root causes against Claude's 28 (174 of them new to Claude, 15 shared with Claude), at 0 USD on the plan, in 22 min and about 472,000 tokens per run, with a 16.1% false-alarm rate (Claude: 0 of 28). But it found only 5 of the 10 BLOCKING or SERIOUS root causes (Claude found 8), it missed the one done-check blocker (A1) and the Secrets blocker only counts because the orchestrator ran the canary, and 66.2% of its REAL rows had their severity lowered (BLOCKING labels were right 2 times out of 78). Per BLOCKING or SERIOUS root cause found, one astra run costs about 95,000 audit tokens against Fable's 105,000, so the token price per important find is similar; astra's edge is 0 USD and a wider net of MINOR finds, not a cheaper way to the serious ones. Two of its five important finds are its own (SERIOUS) (the run-note cross-folder install gap and the staged `.env` in the index ref), and that justifies keeping it. A second run adds 27 root causes to run 1 (16.7%) for another 22 min and about 469,000 tokens plus about 228 more judge rows: run-to-run agreement is 76.7%, and the run-only extras are mostly MINOR, so one run is enough. The real cost is the verdict pass, not the audit: 449 astra rows took five Opus judges at most 50 judge-minutes, so for later sweeps judge only astra rows labelled BLOCKING or SERIOUS (292 of 448 in this trial) and take the MINOR rows as a list (all 8 REAL BLOCKING or SERIOUS astra rows were among those 292). This is not "matches Fable", so Lazlo's condition for making astra the main auditor is not met; Fable stays the main stage-end auditor and astra the cheap second pass.

Evidence limits: the judges' tokens and USD are not known to this merge; Claude's plan USD is not available; cross-auditor matching rests on the judges' root-cause slugs. If the orchestrator records a ledger row in `RETRO.md`, the measure is "BLOCKING or SERIOUS root causes found per audit token" and the watch-for-harm signal is "a BLOCKING root cause that only Claude finds again" (A1 here).
